# Demonstração do agente — 3 turnos

## Turno 1

**Cliente:** Hi, my order hasn't arrived yet, where is it?

**Agente:** I understand you’d like to check the status of your order. Could you please provide your order number so I can look into its whereabouts?

## Turno 2

**Cliente:** Actually this product arrived damaged, I want my money back.

**Agente:** I’m sorry to hear the product arrived damaged. Could you share your order number and a brief description of the damage so we can start the refund process?

## Turno 3

**Cliente:** yes that's right

**Agente:** Got it. To proceed with the refund, could you please provide your order number and any photos of the damaged item? This will help us resolve the issue quickly.
