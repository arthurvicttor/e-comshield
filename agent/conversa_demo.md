# Demonstração do agente — 3 turnos

## Turno 1

**Cliente:** Hi, my order hasn't arrived yet, where is it?

**Agente:** I understand you’re asking about the status of your order. Could you please provide your order number so I can check the details for you?

## Turno 2

**Cliente:** Actually this product arrived damaged, I want my money back.

**Agente:** I’m sorry to hear the product arrived damaged. Could you share your order number and the item’s name so we can start the refund process?

## Turno 3

**Cliente:** yes that's right

**Agente:** Great, thank you. To proceed with the refund, could you please provide your order number and the name of the damaged item?
